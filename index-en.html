About Me

I teach how I live.

I'm Lisa, and for me Tai Chi is far more than a form of movement. It's a way of experiencing life more consciously, more vividly, and with more ease.

  • Tai Chi since 2018, discovered in an ashram in Kerala, South India
  • Classes in Erfurt at Yoga Palais and Ekatra, private sessions in person and online
  • Inner calm and deep aliveness, both at once
  • No pressure to perform, at your own rhythm

My journey with Tai Chi began rather unexpectedly in 2018, in an ashram in Kerala, South India. I actually had very different travel plans. I wanted to go to Vietnam to explore my roots on my father's side. But sometimes life leads us to places we didn't know we were looking for.

There I met my Tai Chi teacher and had an experience that changed my perception at its core. For the first time, I felt and understood that energy can be set in motion by the power of my thoughts alone, and that there is so much energy, joy, and aliveness within me. I was fascinated, and from that moment on, Tai Chi never let go of me.

Through Tai Chi I was able to travel to the most beautiful places in the world and practice there, even without a steady job. It has become a firm part of my life. It has taught me to be calmer, to develop more trust in myself and in life, and to stop constantly looking for joy outside myself.

I've learned that it isn't about reaching a goal as quickly as possible, but about enjoying the path itself. That you can't pull on a blade of grass to make it grow faster. And that life gets easier when we stop trying to control and force everything.

Today, Tai Chi brings me inner calm and, at the same time, a deep sense of aliveness. It gives me the freedom to stay curious, to follow my own path, and to let life surprise me with trust, like water finding its way.

These are exactly the experiences I want to share with others. I want to create a space where you can come to rest, discover new energy, become more aware of yourself, and rediscover the joy of movement and of life.

Because for me, Golden Chi isn't about having to achieve anything or master it perfectly. It's about connecting with yourself, finding your own rhythm, and moving through life with more ease, trust, and joy.

"The soft overcomes the hard. Stillness moves more than haste."

Questions

Before We Begin

Do I need experience?

No. Tai Chi and Chi Gong start exactly where you are. The movements are slow, and you practice at your own pace.

How do I sign up for a class?

Through the website of Yoga Palais or Ekatra Erfurt. You book your spot there and find the prices.

Online or in person?

Both. The weekly classes take place in person in Erfurt, and private sessions are available in person or online. An online course for practicing at home is in preparation.

What language does this happen in?

Mostly in German, and in English when needed.

What if the price doesn't work right now?

Reach out. There's flexibility for some situations.

Is the bodywork a medical treatment?

No. Daoist bodywork is an energetic, relaxing form of support. It doesn't replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment and makes no promise of healing.

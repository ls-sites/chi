The Practice

Three paths, one flow: movement, breath, touch.

Three practice forms, one principle: do less, be more present. It's enough to be there and join in.

Tai Chi

太极 · moving stillness

Slow, flowing movements in which body and mind come to rest. You practice at your own pace, with no pressure to perform.

Chi Gong

气功 · breath & strength

Simple exercises that open the breath and let energy flow again. What feels heavy and tired becomes light and mobile again.

Daoist Bodywork

点穴 · acupressure

Gentle work on the body's points and pathways. Deep relaxation, release of what's held, a sense of arriving in your own body.

About Me

I teach how I live.

I'm Lisa, and I teach Tai Chi and Chi Gong in and around Erfurt. Daoist practice is how I live: slowly, attentively, and close to nature.

My classes and sessions are about rest and reconnection with your body, your breath, and nature. That's why I love working with others, with yoga studios in Erfurt and on shared days outdoors.

Questions

Before We Begin

Do I need experience?+

No. Tai Chi and Chi Gong start exactly where you are. The movements are slow, and you practice at your own pace.

How do I sign up for a class?+

Directly through Yoga Palais or Ekatra Erfurt. You'll find prices there too.

Online or in person?+

Both. The weekly classes take place in person in Erfurt, and private sessions are available in person or online. An online course for practicing at home is in preparation.

What language does this happen in?+

Mostly in German, and in English when needed.

What if the price doesn't work right now?+

Reach out. There's flexibility for some situations.

Is the bodywork a medical treatment?+

No. Daoist bodywork is an energetic, relaxing form of support. It doesn't replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment and makes no promise of healing.

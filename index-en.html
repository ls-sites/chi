Questions

Before We Begin

Do I need experience?+

No. Tai Chi and Chi Gong start exactly where you are. It's not about ability, it's about practice.

Online or in person?+

Both. The ongoing practice happens online, so it stays location independent, for you and for me. In-person meetings happen at retreats and whenever we cross paths in Europe or Bali.

What language does this happen in?+

In English or German, whichever works best for you.

What if the price doesn't work right now?+

Reach out. Payment plans are available, and there's flexibility for some situations. This shouldn't fail because of money.

Is the bodywork a medical treatment?+

No. Daoist acupressure is an energetic, relaxing form of support. It doesn't replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment and makes no promise of healing.

A first conversation: calm and no commitment.

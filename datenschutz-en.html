To prevent spam, FormSubmit shows a Google reCAPTCHA check before sending (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland); technical data such as your IP address may be transmitted to Google. Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU, in particular the USA. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the video you requested). More about FormSubmit: formsubmit.co.
